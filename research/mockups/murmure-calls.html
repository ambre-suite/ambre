Murmure: channels, calls, screen share

Non-normative. Snapshot of a design discussion held on 2026-11-21. The decisions it led to live in the Murmure specs (specs/5-Murmure/); where this page and a spec disagree, the spec wins. This file is frozen: it is not updated when the specs change. One addition since, on 2026-11-26: the screen that creates or joins a space, and the space switcher on phone (the “New space” control).

Discussion mockup. Base: the Murmure specs of the time (three columns, thread on the right, call started from a channel, video above the composer). The table at the bottom gives the outcome of each point; orange marks a change to those specs.

What the mockup settles

TopicRuleOutcome
Message modelOnly one: a thread is a chain of replies on any message, opened on the right. “Also send to #channel” under the thread composer.in spec
OrganisationSpaces → categories → channels, then private channels, direct messages, channels to join. No icon rail.in spec
Space switcherAt the top of the sidebar. The sidebar shows one space at a time. Ctrl 1…Ctrl 9 switch spaces. DMs stay shared across all spaces.adopted
Create or join a spaceThe last row of the switcher opens one dialog with both paths, create on top, join below: no tab hides one of them. Create takes a name and a mark: initials drawn from the name (editable, two letters) on a colour of the switcher palette. A blank name keeps “Create” off (MURMURE-583). The new space opens selected, with no channel, and offers “Create a channel” and “Invite people” (MURMURE-584, MURMURE-585). Join takes the invite link the owner sent; the mark of the inviter's key it carries shows before the join (SYNC-236). A cut link and a link that no longer opens a space each say what to do next.proposed
Space switcher on phoneThe channel list, home of the stack, takes the space as its title: mark, name, chevron. A tap opens a bottom sheet with the spaces and “Create or join a space”, within reach of the thumb. Create or join stacks as a full page, with back. No space control in a channel: the stack already says where you are.proposed
Collapsible categoriesEach sidebar group folds: categories, Private, Direct messages, To join. Folded, it keeps the open channel, a channel with a call and the channels that mention you visible; the other unread channels sum up as a dot and a count. The fold state follows the account (Murmure settings), never the space catalogue.adopted; fold state follows the account
Live call in the sidebarA channel with a call shows a green phone and the participants' avatars; under it, the timer and “Join”. Join in one click, with no permanent room.adopted, reduced: icon and count, no names; avatars only for the shown channel (ZK-024)
Call controlsIn the channel header, grouped on a green background with the timer. No floating bar at the bottom in the Zoom style.in spec
Two automatic layoutsGrid (3×3, pages) by default. Scene as soon as a screen arrives or someone pins: the subject takes the space, faces move to a side strip (bottom strip on phone). No “gallery / speaker” menu.adopted
A screen share switches to sceneA received screen enlarges the call into the scene; “shrink” returns to the call band with the screen alone. The sharer sees a “You are sharing” bar and never their own screen.adopted
Call bubbleLeaving the call's channel (another channel, Prose, Trame, Toile) leaves a floating bubble: channel, timer, speaking faces, mic, camera, screen, back to the channel, leave. It can be dragged and snaps to the corners. It publishes the area it covers (story “The floating cover”). Click on the faces: a mini video of who is speaking, or of the shared screen.adopted
Present an Ambre documentNo screen share for an Ambre document: “Present” opens the document for everyone, in follow mode (the view follows the presenter's). Everyone keeps their own context panel, selection and tools. Editing or scrolling stops following; a click on the follow bar resumes. The call turns into a bubble, on the side opposite the panel. Screen share stays for what is not in Ambre.adopted for v1: document members only; others get “Invite as viewer”, screen share meanwhile
Fill gridYour tile becomes the last cell of the grid, whose column count follows the shape of the area (4×3 on a wide screen). Pages of eleven: the video server must send eleven streams, not nine (CALL-041, budget).adopted; CALL-041 raised to 11 on desktop, 9 on phone; replaces MURMURE-652
Band page sizesThe page size follows the room each layout has: five tiles in the desktop call band, three on phone, four in the scene strip. Order of arrival; arrows, dots and “1–5 of 20”; Page ↑ Page ↓ on the keyboard. A speaker on another page lights the arrow toward them.adopted; replaces MURMURE-654
PhoneOutside the call's channel: a green pill under the header. In the scene: screen at the top, faces in a horizontal strip, round controls at the bottom (the only place where controls leave the header: the thumb).adopted; stack navigation, Murmure-only bottom tab bar
Phone channel listThe list is the home screen; a channel stacks on it, a thread on the channel; back is the native gesture. A swipe drawer was rejected: it would fight the call band's horizontal scroll.adopted
Presence in the sidebarThe participant list comes from the request to the relay (ZK-024), today limited to the shown channel. Extending it to the sidebar widens what the relay learns: to be decided.settled: icon and count only
Video in the bubbleVideos are drawn by the canvas (CALL-015). In Prose, the bubble's mini video must go through the Prose render runtime. The call survives the app switch (the call lives in the page, one router): to check in code.adopted; goes through the render runtime
